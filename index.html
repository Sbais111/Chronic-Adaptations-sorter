Master all 30 chronic adaptations

Students first sort every adaptation into its training category. They then match each adaptation to its description and finally to its performance benefit.

How it works

  1. Stage 1: drag all adaptation cards into the correct category.
  2. Stage 2: match adaptations to their descriptions.
  3. Stage 3: match adaptations to their benefits.
  4. Cards and matching choices are randomized every game.
  5. On touch devices, tap a card and then tap a category.

Increased a-vO₂ difference is accepted as either Aerobic Cardiovascular or Aerobic Muscular.
